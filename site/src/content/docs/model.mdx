The whole robot, part by part, in your browser. Turn it around, see through the printed frame to the servos, computer and battery, put it together in the order it would be built, and play simple movements while every servo's load is shown live. It is Marvin Bot's [3-D page](https://kranthie.github.io/marvin-bot/guide/00-3d-model/) on the mini's model.

## What you are looking at

This model is not a drawing someone made by hand. The design lives in code: `description/robot.yaml` lists every joint and servo, the code-CAD in `cad/` generates the printed parts, and `tools/build_urdf.py` turns all of it into the robot model the simulators read. This page loads that same generated model, rebuilt from the repository every time the site is published: 28 rigid bodies (links) joined by 27 servo-driven joints, wrists and pincer grippers included. Every printed part is drawn in code-CAD, with its screw holes, bearing seats, cable passages and the clip-on shells' hooks and screw bosses; the bought electronics (computer, battery, power board, camera) are outline boxes at their sizes and masses.

Four tabs on the left: **Layers** (what is it made of?), **Assembly** (how is it built?), **Skills** (how does it move?) and **Joints** (pose it by hand).

## Playground

:::note[Try this]
1. <b>Layers:</b> press *X-ray: servos*: 23 of them, six per leg, four per arm, waist and neck. The gold-coded ones in "by layer" colours are the STS3250s (legs and shoulder pitch), the blue ones the STS3215s.
2. <b>Skills:</b> play *Step up 5 cm* and watch the knee of the leg on the step as it lifts the whole body.
3. <b>Joints:</b> press *Arms forward*: the shoulder pitch servos hold both arms straight out, which is why they are the stronger STS3250 (`docs/decisions.md`, 2026-11-09).
:::

The loads are quasi-static: gravity only, with the weight shared between the feet on the ground, no accelerations or impacts. The project's own checks are in MuJoCo (`sim/mujoco/check_torques.py` for held poses, `check_policy.py` for walking), with the servo model.
